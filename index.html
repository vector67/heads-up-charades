Settings
Tilt uses your phone's motion sensor. Tap shows buttons instead, for when a friend holds the phone or there's no sensor.
How to play
  1. Pick a deck, then hold the phone sideways on your forehead, screen facing your friends.
  2. Your friends act out or describe the word.
  3. Tilt down when you get it right ✅
  4. Tilt up to pass ⏭️
  5. Get as many as you can before the timer runs out.
