# Heads Up Charades

A mobile Heads Up! clone in a single `index.html`, with no build step and no dependencies.

**Play:** https://vector67.github.io/heads-up-charades/

## How to play

1. Pick a deck, then hold the phone sideways on your forehead with the screen facing your friends.
2. The 60-second round starts as soon as the phone is upright on your forehead.
3. **Tilt down** when you get it right ✅. **Tilt up** to pass ⏭️.
4. When time runs out you see your score and every card, right or missed.

Other features:

- 10 built-in decks, plus custom decks you can make yourself (saved on your device).
- Round length of 30, 60, 90 or 120 seconds.
- **Tap** mode with on-screen Pass/Correct buttons, for devices without a motion sensor.
- On a computer, the arrow keys work: ↓ is correct, ↑ is pass, Esc quits.
- Add `?debug` to the URL to show live sensor readings.

On iPhone, Safari asks for permission to use motion and orientation the first time. Tap **Allow**.
